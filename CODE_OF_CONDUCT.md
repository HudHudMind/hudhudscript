# Hudhud Script Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Hudhud Script is built as an open, welcoming, respectful, and collaborative open-source community.

We are committed to providing a safe and constructive environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, education, technical background, age, nationality, ethnicity, language, disability, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, philosophy, socio-economic status, or any other personal characteristic.

Everyone who participates in good faith should be able to contribute, ask questions, learn, discuss ideas, and disagree without being humiliated or treated as inferior.

## Respect, Kindness, and Technical Humility

Technical knowledge does not give anyone the right to look down on another person.

In the Hudhud Script community, we expect contributors to communicate with courtesy, patience, and respect.

When explaining something:

* Be helpful and explanatory rather than dismissive.
* Do not talk down to people.
* Do not belittle someone because they know less than you.
* Do not mock questions for being basic, obvious, or beginner-level.
* Do not use technical knowledge, seniority, education, job title, or experience as a way to establish superiority over another contributor.
* Explain why something is wrong instead of simply telling someone that they are wrong.
* When possible, help people understand how they can improve their contribution.
* Criticize code, ideas, designs, and technical decisions, not the person who created them.
* Assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.

A healthy technical community is one where experienced developers help others grow rather than making them afraid to participate.

## Encouraged Behavior

Examples of behavior we encourage include:

* Treating other people with empathy, patience, and kindness.
* Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and technical approaches.
* Giving constructive and actionable feedback.
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
* Asking questions when something is unclear.
* Helping newcomers understand the project.
* Sharing knowledge without arrogance or condescension.
* Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them.
* Focusing disagreements on technical issues rather than personalities.
* Giving people room to learn.
* Recognizing that people communicate differently across cultures and native languages.
* Working toward solutions that benefit the project and its community.

Hudhud Script is a multilingual project. Contributors should remember that another participant may be communicating in a language that is not their first language. Grammar, vocabulary, or communication style should never be used as a reason to ridicule or dismiss someone.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable within the Hudhud Script community:

* Harassment, intimidation, or threats.
* Personal attacks.
* Insults or deliberately derogatory comments.
* Persistent trolling or disruptive behavior.
* Racist, sexist, discriminatory, or dehumanizing language.
* Unwanted sexual language, imagery, attention, or advances.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Deliberately humiliating another contributor.
* Mocking someone because of their technical ability or lack of experience.
* Treating beginners as inferior.
* Condescending, patronizing, or intentionally dismissive communication.
* Using one's position, expertise, maintainer status, professional background, or seniority to intimidate others.
* Repeatedly responding to reasonable questions with ridicule rather than explanation.
* Attacking a person's intelligence instead of discussing their code or ideas.
* Gatekeeping participation based on language, education, professional credentials, or background.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered harmful or inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Technical Disagreements

Disagreement is a normal and useful part of software development.

Strong technical criticism is allowed and encouraged when it helps improve the project. However, criticism must remain focused on the work.

For example:

**Acceptable:**

> This implementation may cause unnecessary allocations. We could avoid them by changing the data structure.

**Not acceptable:**

> Anyone who writes code like this clearly does not understand programming.

Being technically correct does not excuse disrespectful behavior.

Maintainers and experienced contributors are expected to set an example by explaining decisions and technical objections clearly whenever reasonably possible.

## New Contributors

Questions from new contributors are welcome.

No contributor should be made to feel embarrassed for not knowing something.

Hudhud Script aims to encourage experimentation, learning, and new approaches to programming. People entering the project may come from very different backgrounds and levels of experience.

When someone makes a mistake, our preferred response is to explain the problem and help them understand the solution.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for applying these standards fairly and consistently.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, maintainers may:

1. Ask a participant to change their behavior.
2. Issue a formal warning.
3. Temporarily restrict participation.
4. Temporarily ban a participant.
5. Permanently remove a participant from project spaces.

Serious harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained abusive behavior may result in immediate removal.

Maintainer status does not place anyone above this Code of Conduct. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards as every other participant.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Hudhud Script project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, community platforms, communication channels, events, and other official project spaces.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing Hudhud Script in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at:

**[onur@hudhudscript.com](mailto:onur@hudhudscript.com)**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information about an incident will only be shared when necessary to investigate the report, protect participants, or enforce this Code of Conduct.

Anyone responsible for reviewing or enforcing a report must avoid conflicts of interest and treat all involved parties fairly.

## Our Principle

We want Hudhud Script to be a community where people can say:

> **"I don't know. Can you explain it?"**

without being afraid of being mocked.

Knowledge should be shared, not used as a tool for superiority.

The quality of an open-source community is measured not only by the quality of its code, but also by how its members treat one another.
